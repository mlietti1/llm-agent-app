# Petra Vlhová

> Slovak alpine ski racer, Olympic slalom champion and 2021 overall World Cup winner.

## At a glance
- **Born:** June 13, 1995, Liptovský Mikuláš, Slovakia
- **Nationality:** Slovak; **height:** 1.80 m
- **Club:** Vojenské športové centrum Dukla Banská Bystrica; associated with Ski Team Vlha
- **World Cup debut:** December 29, 2012
- **Disciplines:** Slalom, giant slalom, super-G, downhill and combined

## Background
Vlhová grew up in the Liptov region of northern Slovakia and began skiing as a child. She progressed through junior competition before making her World Cup debut at 17. She became one of the leading technical racers of her generation, particularly in slalom and giant slalom.

## Career highlights
At the 2019 World Championships in Åre, Vlhová won giant slalom gold, combined silver and slalom bronze. In 2021 she won the overall women's World Cup title, becoming the first Slovak skier to claim the overall crystal globe.

At Beijing 2022, she won Olympic slalom gold. After a difficult first run, a decisive second run secured Slovakia's first Olympic alpine skiing gold medal.

In January 2024 she suffered a serious knee injury during a World Cup giant slalom at Jasná. Surgery and rehabilitation kept her out of the remainder of that season and subsequent competition periods. She did not compete at Milano Cortina 2026 while continuing her recovery.

## Milestones
- **2012:** World Cup debut.
- **2015:** First World Cup victory.
- **2019:** World giant slalom champion.
- **2021:** Overall World Cup champion.
- **2022:** Olympic slalom gold.
- **2024:** Serious knee injury at Jasná and start of rehabilitation.

## Personal life and legacy
Vlhová remains closely associated with her home region of Liptov. Her Olympic gold, world title, overall World Cup crown and 31 World Cup victories made her a landmark athlete in Slovak sport and a major rival in women's technical skiing.
