# Federica Brignone

> Italian alpine ski racer, two-time overall World Cup champion and double Olympic gold medalist at Milano Cortina 2026.

## At a glance
- **Born:** July 14, 1990, Milan, Italy
- **Nationality:** Italian; **height:** 1.68 m
- **Club:** C.S. Carabinieri
- **World Cup debut:** December 28, 2007
- **Disciplines:** Giant slalom, super-G, downhill, slalom and combined
- **Nickname:** La Tigre

## Background
Brignone was born in Milan and grew up in the Aosta Valley. Her mother, Maria Rosa Quario, was an alpine ski racer and later a sports journalist. Brignone developed through the Italian junior system and made her World Cup debut at 17.

## Career highlights
Brignone first established herself as a giant slalom specialist, then developed into a versatile racer capable of winning in speed events and combined. In 2020 she became the first Italian woman to win the overall women's World Cup title, also taking the giant slalom and combined globes. She won the overall title again in 2025.

At the World Championships, she won combined gold in 2023 and giant slalom gold in 2025. She also has silver medals from 2011 and 2021.

At Milano Cortina 2026, Brignone won Olympic gold in both super-G and giant slalom on home snow. She had suffered a serious injury in 2025 and made a demanding return to competition before the Games.

## World Championships
Her major championship results include silver in giant slalom (2011 and 2021), gold in combined (2023), and gold in giant slalom (2025). The English Wikipedia profile lists five World Championship medals overall.

## Milestones
- **2007:** World Cup debut.
- **2020:** First Italian woman to win the overall World Cup.
- **2023:** World combined champion.
- **2025:** Second overall World Cup title and world giant slalom gold.
- **2026:** Two Olympic gold medals at Milano Cortina.

## Personal interests and legacy
Brignone is closely connected to the Aosta Valley and has supported environmental and mountain-protection initiatives. Her longevity, technical range and success in both technical and speed events make her one of Italy's greatest alpine skiers.
