# Michelle Gisin

> Swiss alpine ski racer and two-time Olympic champion in the alpine combined, winning the event at PyeongChang 2018 and Beijing 2022.

## At a glance
- **Full name:** Michelle Géraldine Gisin
- **Born:** December 5, 1993, Samedan, Graubünden, Switzerland
- **Nationality:** Swiss; **height:** 1.74 m
- **World Cup debut:** December 29, 2012
- **Disciplines:** Slalom, giant slalom, super-G, downhill and combined
- **Family:** Sister of Olympic downhill champion Dominique Gisin
- **Spouse:** Italian alpine skier Luca De Aliprandini

## Background
Gisin was born in Samedan and grew up in a family closely connected to skiing. Her sister Dominique won Olympic downhill gold at Sochi 2014. Michelle developed through Swiss junior racing and made her World Cup debut in 2012, gradually establishing herself as a versatile all-rounder.

## Career highlights
Gisin's breakthrough came at PyeongChang 2018, where she won Olympic gold in alpine combined. At Beijing 2022 she defended that title, becoming the first woman to win the Olympic combined at consecutive Games. She also earned bronze in super-G at Beijing, taking her Olympic medal total to three.

At the World Championships, she won combined silver at St. Moritz 2017 and combined bronze at Cortina d'Ampezzo 2021. Her career is notable for strong performances across both technical and speed disciplines.

## Milestones
- **2012:** World Cup debut.
- **2017:** World Championship combined silver.
- **2018:** Olympic combined gold.
- **2021:** World Championship combined bronze; third overall in World Cup.
- **2022:** Defended Olympic combined gold and won super-G bronze.

## Personal life and legacy
Gisin comes from a prominent Swiss skiing family. Her siblings Dominique and Marc were also involved in alpine skiing. She married Italian racer Luca De Aliprandini. Her consecutive Olympic combined titles and success across disciplines make her one of Switzerland's leading all-round ski racers of the modern era.
