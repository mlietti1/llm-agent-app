# Sofia Goggia

> Italian alpine ski racer and Olympic downhill champion, renowned for her attacking style and speed-event performances.

## At a glance
- **Born:** November 15, 1992, Bergamo, Italy
- **Nationality:** Italian; **height:** about 1.70 m
- **Club:** G.S. Fiamme Gialle
- **World Cup debut:** December 28, 2011
- **Specialties:** Downhill and super-G; also raced giant slalom and combined

## Background
Born in Bergamo, Goggia progressed through the Italian racing system and made her World Cup debut in 2011. After competing across several disciplines early in her career, she became one of the circuit's leading downhill and super-G specialists.

## Career highlights
Goggia emerged as a major international contender in 2016–17, earning podiums across disciplines and finishing third in the overall World Cup. At PyeongChang 2018 she won Olympic downhill gold, becoming the first Italian woman to win Olympic gold in the event.

She won the World Cup downhill title in 2018, 2021, 2022 and 2023. At Beijing 2022, after returning from a serious knee injury shortly before the Games, she won downhill silver. At Milano Cortina 2026 she added downhill bronze, becoming a three-time Olympic medalist in the event across three consecutive Games.

## Milestones
- **2011:** World Cup debut.
- **2017:** Third overall; World Championship super-G bronze.
- **2018:** Olympic downhill gold and first downhill World Cup title.
- **2019:** World Championship downhill silver.
- **2021–23:** Three further downhill titles.
- **2026:** Olympic downhill bronze.

## Personal life and legacy
Goggia is a prominent figure in Italian sport, admired for her aggressive racing and determination through injuries. Her three Olympic downhill medals and four discipline titles make her one of the defining downhill racers of her generation.
