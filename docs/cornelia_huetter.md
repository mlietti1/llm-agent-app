# Cornelia Hütter

> Austrian alpine ski racer specializing in downhill and super-G, and winner of the 2024 women's World Cup downhill title.

## At a glance
- **Born:** October 29, 1992, Graz, Styria, Austria
- **Nationality:** Austrian; **height:** 1.71 m
- **Club:** SV St. Radegund
- **World Cup debut:** December 2, 2011
- **Disciplines:** Downhill, super-G and combined
- **Equipment:** Head skis and boots, according to her FIS profile

## Background
Hütter was born in Graz and progressed through Austria's alpine racing system. She made her World Cup debut in December 2011 at age 19 and developed into a regular contender in the women's speed events.

## Career highlights
Hütter won her first World Cup race in 2016. At the 2017 World Championships in St. Moritz, she earned bronze in super-G, an important milestone in her career.

At Beijing 2022 she won Olympic bronze in super-G, her first Olympic medal. Her strongest season to date was 2023–24, when she claimed the women's World Cup downhill discipline title and finished fifth overall.

She continued to add podiums in later seasons. FIS results show her racing in 2025–26, with additional World Cup podium finishes in March 2026.
