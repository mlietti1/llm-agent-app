# Mikaela Shiffrin

> **Mikaela Pauline Shiffrin** is an American alpine ski racer widely regarded as one of the greatest alpine skiers of all time. She is especially dominant in slalom and holds the all-time record for World Cup race victories.

## At a glance

- **Full name:** Mikaela Pauline Shiffrin
- **Born:** March 13, 1995
- **Birthplace:** Vail, Colorado, United States
- **Nationality:** American
- **Height:** 170 cm (5 ft 7 in)
- **Sport:** Alpine skiing
- **Club/academy:** Burke Mountain Academy
- **World Cup debut:** March 11, 2011, at age 15
- **Main disciplines:** Slalom, giant slalom, super-G, downhill, and combined
- **Olympic appearances:** 2014, 2018, 2022, and 2026

## Why she is notable

Shiffrin has achieved a series of historic milestones in alpine skiing:

- She has the most FIS Alpine Ski World Cup race wins of any skier, male or female, according to the referenced profile.
- She became the first alpine skier to reach **100 World Cup victories**.
- She is a **three-time Olympic gold medalist** and has won four Olympic medals in total.
- She has won **15 World Championship medals**, including **eight gold medals**.
- She has won the overall World Cup title six times and the women's slalom discipline title nine times.
- At 18 years and 345 days, she became the youngest Olympic slalom champion in history at the 2014 Sochi Games.

## Early life and family

Shiffrin was born in Vail, Colorado, and grew up in a family with a strong skiing background. Her parents, Jeff and Eileen Shiffrin, were both ski racers. She developed her skiing at a young age and attended Burke Mountain Academy, a school known for its ski-racing program. Her brother, Taylor, also pursued skiing.

## Career overview

### World Cup breakthrough

Shiffrin made her World Cup debut in March 2011. Her first World Cup victory came in Åre, Sweden, when she was 17. She quickly became one of the leading technical skiers on the circuit, particularly in slalom, while also building results in giant slalom and speed events.

### Olympic Games

Shiffrin has competed at four Winter Olympics:

| Year | Host | Key results |
|---|---|---|
| 2014 | Sochi, Russia | Gold in women's slalom; fifth in giant slalom |
| 2018 | PyeongChang, South Korea | Gold in giant slalom; silver in combined; fourth in slalom |
| 2022 | Beijing, China | No individual medals; fourth in the mixed team event |
| 2026 | Milano Cortina, Italy | Gold in women's slalom; 11th in giant slalom; fourth in team combined |

Her 2014 slalom gold made her the youngest Olympic champion in the event at the time. Her 2026 slalom victory added a third Olympic gold medal.

### World Alpine Ski Championships

Shiffrin has earned **15 World Championship medals: eight gold, four silver, and three bronze**. Her gold medals span multiple events and championships:

- **2013, Schladming:** Slalom
- **2015, Beaver Creek:** Slalom
- **2017, St. Moritz:** Slalom
- **2019, Åre:** Slalom and super-G
- **2021, Cortina d'Ampezzo:** Alpine combined
- **2023, Méribel:** Giant slalom
- **2025, Saalbach:** Team combined

Her World Championship record demonstrates that her success extends beyond her signature slalom event.

## World Cup statistics and titles

The referenced Wikipedia profile lists the following career totals:

- **World Cup wins:** 110
- **World Cup podiums:** 168
- **Overall World Cup titles:** 6 — 2017, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023, and 2026
- **Slalom discipline titles:** 9 — 2013, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2023, 2024, and 2026
- **Giant slalom discipline titles:** 2 — 2019 and 2023
- **Super-G discipline titles:** 1 — 2019

Slalom is her strongest event, but her victories in giant slalom and speed disciplines show unusual versatility for a skier with such a large technical-event record.

## Selected career milestones

- **2011:** Made her World Cup debut at age 15.
- **2013:** Won her first World Championship gold medal, in slalom.
- **2014:** Won Olympic slalom gold at Sochi.
- **2018:** Won Olympic giant slalom gold and combined silver at PyeongChang.
- **2019:** Won the overall World Cup title and World Championship gold in both slalom and super-G.
- **2022–23:** Broke the previous record for all-time World Cup wins and won five medals across five races at the 2023 World Championships.
- **2023:** Was named to *Time* magazine's list of the 100 most influential people.
- **2025:** Won her eighth World Championship gold, in team combined, bringing her World Championship medal total to 15.
- **2026:** Won Olympic gold in women's slalom at Milano Cortina and claimed another overall World Cup title.

## Personal interests and activities outside skiing

Beyond competition, Shiffrin plays guitar and piano and enjoys tennis and windsurfing. She has also supported athlete well-being and access to winter sports.

- In 2020, she launched the **Jeff Shiffrin Athlete Resiliency Fund**, named in memory of her father, to help athletes facing financial or training-related challenges.
- After her 100th World Cup win, she pledged to raise money for **Share Winter**, an organization that helps young people access skiing and snowboarding.

## Legacy

Shiffrin's legacy rests on both longevity and range. Her record number of World Cup victories, six overall titles, nine slalom titles, three Olympic gold medals, and 15 World Championship medals place her among the defining athletes in alpine skiing history. While slalom remains her signature discipline, her achievements across technical and speed events underline her exceptional adaptability.
